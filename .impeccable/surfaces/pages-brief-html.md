## Scope

The brief page (reading, generating, error). Mode: Read. Popup, welcome and settings keep the old look until the user approves this world; Settings gained a "Look & language" section (scene, time preview, English/Arabic).

## Audience and job

Anyone starting a workday in a browser, non-technical included, in English or Arabic. Caught up in two minutes, knows what to do first.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The brief opens on a living pixel-art window into the hour it was written (place, time of day, weather) drawn in code. Refuses the card dashboard and the cream editorial column.

OWN-WORLD: 3px-pixel dioramas (desk by a window, Riyadh rooftop, lakeside cabin, town street) whose sky tints the page; the scene dithers into a time-tinted ground. Thmanyah Serif Display greets, Thmanyah Sans reads, Zanjabeel greets in Arabic. Pixel-stepped corners, 9×9 pixel icons, dotted pixel rules. No cards, no glass.

STORY: Scene and a greeting for the hour; one headline and summary; then push forward, to-dos, updates, the day, people, looking ahead; follow-ups in the Ask bar.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed scene (~432px desktop, 288px phone). Over the sky: wordmark, last seven days, scene/copy/rewrite/settings. Greeting in the quiet corner (the scene mirrors in Arabic). Headline under the dither seam. Ask bar fixed at the bottom.

FORM: Cozy pixel diorama, pinned by the user's brief; seed key: pinned-pixel-diorama.

SIGNATURE: While the brief is written, a light in the scene comes on for each app read (laptop screen, string lights, lanterns, cabin windows, shop windows). Days change with a Bayer dither dissolve. Weather from the brief rains or snows in the scene.

## Unresolved

The user picks one of the four scenes; the others stay selectable. Thmanyah's licence forbids shipping extractable font files without permission.
